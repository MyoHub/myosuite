MyoSuite's first SuperMimic adaptation (myosuite/learning/supermimic, MyoSuite PR #573, replaced by
this project) adapted parts of SuperMimic-Reproduced (https://github.com/CuboiLeo/SuperMimic-Reproduced),
an independent reproduction of SuperMimic (Liao et al., arXiv:2610.28480): the world-model state layout
and explicit integration, and the imagined actor-critic objective with soft survival weights and
lambda-return critic targets. This project is built on muscle-supermimic's port of supermimic_vibed
instead (see NOTICE-muscle-supermimic.txt); the reproduction's notice stays with the method:
