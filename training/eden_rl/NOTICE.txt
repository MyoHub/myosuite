This package adapts reinforcement learning architectures, optimizers, latent
estimators, and adversarial motion priors from external open-source research
and repositories for musculoskeletal control in MyoSuite, in accordance with
docs/wiki/training-guidelines.md ("Attribution and licensing"):

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Eden (https://github.com/embodied-ai-nexus/Eden)
   Copyright 2025-2026 Embodied AI Nexus (Eden Contributors).
   Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0
   (http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

   Adapted upstream modules:
   - packages/eden/src/eden/learning/wrappers/rsl_rl/simba/{networks,model}.py
   - packages/eden/src/eden/learning/wrappers/rsl_rl/muon_ppo/ppo.py
   - packages/eden/src/eden/learning/wrappers/rsl_rl/him/{estimator,ppo}.py
   - packages/eden/src/eden/learning/wrappers/rsl_rl/amp_ppo/{amp,ppo}.py

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SimBaV2 (https://github.com/DAVIAN-Robotics/SimbaV2) & Weave (https://github.com/xiaohu-art/Weave)
   Official SimBaV2 repository licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0
   (http://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).

   Paper citation:
   Lee, H., Hwang, D., Kim, D., Kim, H., Tai, J. J., Subramanian, K.,
   Wurman, P. R., Choo, J., Stone, P., & Seno, T. (2025).
   "Hyperspherical Normalization for Scalable Deep Reinforcement Learning."
   arXiv:2502.15280 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.15280).

   Adapted components in eden_rl/simba.py:
   - Hyperspherical residual blocks (l2normalize, Scaler, HyperDense,
     HyperEmbedder, HyperMLP, HyperLERPBlock, SimBa) and per-group observation
     GroupEncoder / SimBaModel.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Muon Optimizer (https://github.com/KellerJordan/Muon; PyTorch torch.optim.Muon)
   Copyright (c) 2024 Keller Jordan and contributors (MIT License); PyTorch
   implementation licensed under BSD-3-Clause.

   Paper/technical report citation:
   Jordan, K., Jin, Y., Boza, V., Jiacheng, Y., Cecista, F., Newhouse, L., &
   Bernstein, J. (2024). "Muon: An optimizer for hidden layers in neural
   networks." https://github.com/KellerJordan/Muon

   Adapted components in eden_rl/muon_ppo.py:
   - MuonAdamW parameter routing (2-D matrices to torch.optim.Muon with
     adjust_lr_fn="match_rms_adamw", 1-D/scalar parameters to AdamW) and MuonPPO.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HIMLoco (https://github.com/InternRobotics/HIMLoco) & OpenHomie (https://github.com/OpenRobotLab/HomieRL)
   Copyright (c) 2024 InternRobotics / OpenRobotLab contributors.
   Licensed under the MIT License:

   Permission is hereby granted, free of charge, to any person obtaining a copy

   in the Software without restriction, including without limitation the rights
   to use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and/or sell
   copies of the Software, and to permit persons to whom the Software is

   The above copyright notice and this permission notice shall be included in
   all copies or substantial portions of the Software.

   Paper citation:
   Long, J., Wang, Z., Li, Q., Cao, L., Gao, J., & Pang, J. (2024).
   "Hybrid Internal Model: Learning Agile Legged Locomotion with Simulated
   Robot Response." International Conference on Learning Representations
   (ICLR 2024), arXiv:2312.11460 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.11460).

   Adapted components in eden_rl/him.py:
   - Sinkhorn-Knopp prototype assignment (sinkhorn), SwAV + target regression
     proprioceptive history encoder (HIMEstimator), and bilateral mirror losses.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Adversarial Motion Priors (AMP) & HUSKY (https://github.com/TeleHuman/humanoid_skateboarding)
   Paper citation:
   Peng, X. B., Ma, Z., Abbeel, P., Levine, S., & Kanazawa, A. (2021).
   "AMP: Adversarial Motion Priors for Stylized Physics-Based Character Control."
   ACM Transactions on Graphics (SIGGRAPH 2021), 40(4), Article 144,
   arXiv:2104.02180 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.02180).

   Adapted components in eden_rl/amp.py:
   - Continuous-time motion window sampler (AmpMotionSampler), policy ring
     buffer (AmpReplayBuffer), running FrameNormalizer, spectral-norm LSGAN +
     WGAN-GP discriminator (AmpDiscriminator), and AmpPPO.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MyoSuite-Specific Implementations (Copyright (c) MyoSuite Authors, Apache-2.0):
- eden_rl/genesis_bridge.py: GenesisMuscleBridge coupling myo_sim/myosuite Hill
  muscle dynamics and stripped joint-torque MjSpecs with Genesis RigidEntity.
- eden_rl/simba.py: rebuild_simba_trunk, register_simba_rslrl_loader,
  load_simba_policy, and export_simba_to_onnx integrating SimBa checkpoints with
  myosuite.utils.rslrl_policy and myosuite.utils.export_onnx.
- eden_rl/him.py: Generalized target_dim physiological state regression (e.g.
  3CC-r muscle fatigue and sarcopenia force factors) and ONNX-exportable HIMActor.
- eden_rl/amp.py: Direct ingestion of MyoSuite myoMimic-v0 refitted .npz clips
  (qpos slices + fps/dt).
- eden_rl/train.py: CLI and runner config overlay for MyoSuite make_env(..., backend="mjlab").
