# Asset credits

## `myofullbody.skn`

- **What:** MuJoCo binary skin (`.skn`) of the human body surface: 14,517 vertices with UVs,
  26,756 triangles, and 58 bones.
- **Rig:** the bones are bound to the bodies of the MyoSuite full-body model
  ([myo_sim](https://github.com/MyoHub/myo_sim), `myoMimicFullbody-v0`).
- **Contributed by:** Vittorio Caggiano, for the MyoSuite authors.
- **Original body mesh:** _to be confirmed by the contributor (author, source URL and license)._
- **License:** distributed with MyoSuite under the repository's Apache-2.0 license
  ([LICENSE](../../../LICENSE)), subject to the original mesh's license above.
- **Used by:** `myosuite.viz.skin` (`load_skn("fullbody")`) and `scripts/render_blender.py --skin fullbody`.
